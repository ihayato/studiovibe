Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy for Dopakuri! (the "App"). The same text appears in the App under "About This App". The Japanese version is the original.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nihon no Inaka ha Shihonshugi no Frontier da LLC ("we", "us" or "our") handles your information in the game app "Dopakuri!" (the "App"). The controller of the personal data described below is Nihon no Inaka ha Shihonshugi no Frontier da LLC (registered name: 合同会社日本の田舎は資本主義のフロンティアだ), a godo kaisha (limited liability company) organized in Japan. You can contact us at nubonba@gmail.com or through our contact page (https://vibe.co.jp/contact). We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK; please contact us directly.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects and sends only the following information.

  • Leaderboard information (only if you choose a Player Name): only if you choose a Player Name on the Leaderboard, we send a device identifier, your Player Name, the portrait you selected, your records (the deepest night reached), your number of Dawns and the App version to our servers. The device identifier is a random ID generated by the App; it is not derived from your name, your device's serial number or similar. Your Player Name, portrait, records and number of Dawns are shown to other players on the Leaderboard. Only the first few characters of the device identifier are used for display. Records are sent and listed only if you choose a Player Name (even before that, viewing the Leaderboard list requires a connection to our servers; no device identifier is sent then). If you have linked the App with Tsukimidai and rank near the top, we fetch your chosen companion ally and rank (your profile card) from Tsukimidai and show them on the Leaderboard.
  • News ticker (only if you choose a Player Name; you can turn it off in settings): when a named player obtains a UR or a one-of-a-kind item, forges a masterwork, or reaches a milestone night, we send that event with the Player Name and time to our servers and show it to all players in the "news ticker". To recognize events from the same player, the ticker uses a one-way marker derived from the device identifier; the marker remains unchanged if you change your Player Name. To count the total number of spirits defeated by all players, we also send your total number of spirits defeated (your individual count is not shown). If you turn off "Show my name in the ticker" on the Leaderboard name screen, we stop sending these events and counts, and your past events are no longer shown in the ticker.

When you take on a ranked Monthly Trial, we send the times at which you tapped the screen during the trial (time elapsed since the trial began), so that our servers can recount your score. These trial records are deleted 60 days after the trial starts; those kept as evidence for anti-cheat review are deleted 180 days after the trial starts.

When you play in the Card Duel Grand Tournament, we send the numbers of the cards you chose and the order of your moves, so that our servers can recount wins, losses and points. To check for cheating, we keep these records for 60 days (180 days for records used in a check).

  • Tsukimidai link (only if you link): only if you link the App with the members' site "Tsukimidai", we send a linking device identifier (a random ID generated by the App, separate from the Leaderboard device identifier) to our servers and store it together with your Tsukimidai membership. You never enter an email address in the App; approval takes place on the Tsukimidai website. Membership information is handled under Tsukimidai's privacy policy. You can unlink at any time from within the App. While linked, if you have chosen a Player Name, we also send the linking device identifier to the Leaderboard server and pass your monthly Leaderboard rank to Tsukimidai for its overall rankings (we do not pass your Player Name, portrait or record values).
  • Transfer Code (only if you create one): only if you create a Transfer Code, we send a full copy of your save data at that point (including progress, settings, Player Name, and the identifiers used for the Leaderboard and the Tsukimidai link) and your Leaderboard device identifier to our servers, and keep it for you. For this reason, the device identifier is registered on our servers even if you have not chosen a Player Name. When you enter the code on a new device, we send an ID generated for that device. To prevent repeated abusive attempts in a short time, we store a non-reversible value derived from your IP address, grouped by day, and delete it after at most 3 days.
  • Error reports: when an unexpected error occurs in the App, we automatically send its details (the error message, where in the App it occurred, the App version, and the OS type and version) to our servers. We do not include your Player Name, device identifier or save data, and we do not store IP addresses. Identical errors are grouped by day and only counted. They are used only to investigate and fix bugs, and deleted after 90 days.
  • Player Name reports (only if you report): when a named player reports another player's Player Name, we send the reporter's device identifier, the reported player's display ID on the Leaderboard (the first few characters), and the Player Name shown at that time with the date and time, and keep them for 180 days to decide on action. We never tell other players, including the reported player, who made a report.
  • Technical information from communication: when the App communicates with our servers, your IP address and the type of environment you use are, by the nature of the internet, processed on the equipment of our server provider (Cloudflare, Inc.). We may check access logs to investigate outages and prevent abuse, but those logs do not contain raw Player Names or device identifiers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not ask for your name, address, phone number or email address. The App itself has no account registration (linking with Tsukimidai is optional). The App does not read your location, contacts, photos or similar from your device.

We do not show third-party ads (we may show news about our other apps). We do not use advertising identifiers (such as IDFA) or any external analytics tools or trackers. There are no in-app purchases, and we collect no payment information.

3. Purposes

We use the information we collect to provide the Leaderboard, to reflect your rank in Tsukimidai's overall rankings (only if linked), to show the news ticker (unless you turn it off), to deal with falsified records and inappropriate Player Names (including responding to reports), to investigate outages, to respond to inquiries, and to improve the quality of the App.

4. Save Data

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. We send save data to our servers only when you create a Transfer Code. Deleting the App also deletes the save data on your device.

5. Share Feature

The App's "Share Card" feature calls your device's share function and passes it an image and text. You choose where to share and send it yourself; it does not go through our servers. Information on the service you share to is handled under that service's own terms.

6. Sharing With Third Parties and Service Providers

We disclose information only as described in this Policy: to other players through public game features (such as the Leaderboard and the news ticker), to Tsukimidai when you link the App, to Cloudflare, Inc. as our hosting provider, and where disclosure is lawfully required. The App's servers run on services provided by Cloudflare, Inc., and Leaderboard information and save data kept for transfers are stored on its equipment (they may be processed and stored on equipment outside your country, including in the United States and Japan). To let our operators know quickly about records suspected of cheating or about outages, we may send operational notifications to an external notification service (Discord), but those notifications never include information that identifies an individual player, such as Player Names, device identifiers or IP addresses.

7. Retention

Leaderboard information is kept while we provide the Leaderboard, or until you leave the Leaderboard. Past months' Leaderboards are kept as a record (if you leave the Leaderboard, your Player Name and portrait are erased and only the rank remains). Logs of submissions to the Leaderboard are deleted after 60 days, or 180 days for those used to check for cheating. Of the news ticker events, masterwork forgings are deleted after 90 days; UR, one-of-a-kind item and milestone-night events are kept, as a ledger to avoid announcing the same event twice, until you leave the Leaderboard. Save data kept for a transfer is discarded once the import on the new device is complete (within 7 days even if we do not receive the completion signal). Unused save data is discarded 30 days after creation, and when you create a new code, the previous one is discarded at that time. After the save data is discarded, to prevent the same code from being used twice, we keep the code's status, the source and destination device identifiers, a non-reversible digest of the save data, and the date and time for 90 days. If you would like your information deleted, please contact us as described in "8. Deleting Your Information and Contact".

8. Deleting Your Information and Contact

You can delete your Leaderboard information yourself by choosing "Leave the Leaderboard" under "Edit Name & Portrait" on the Leaderboard screen (sending to the Leaderboard also stops at that point). For other players' names, tap their row on the Leaderboard and choose "Hide This Player" or "Report Name".

For requests to access, correct or delete your Leaderboard information, or any other questions about this Policy, please contact nubonba@gmail.com or our contact page (https://vibe.co.jp/contact). To confirm that a record is yours, we may ask for details such as the Player Name shown on the Leaderboard.

9. Children

If you are under 18, please use the App only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Please do not use your real name or other information that could identify you as your Player Name. For questions or requests concerning a child's information, please contact us using the details in section 8.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may change this Policy. When we do, we will announce the new content in advance in the App or on our website.

11. Legal Bases (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Leaderboard, Tsukimidai link and Transfer Code: processing necessary to provide the features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • News ticker and the shared count of spirits defeated: our legitimate interest in providing shared game updates to players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can turn off your contributions as described in section 1.
  • Checking records for cheating, handling reports, keeping security and access logs, and preventing abuse of Transfer Codes: our legitimate interests in keeping the game fair and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Error reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing bugs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Responding to inquiries: our legitimate interest in supporting players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); statutory privacy requests are handled to comply with applicable data protection law.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with an obligation under EU or Member State law, or UK law, respectively (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). Other legal requests are assessed separately.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object as described in section 12.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. You can exercise these rights by contacting nubonba@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (generally within one month). You may also complain to us at nubonba@gmail.com; for UK data protection complaints, we will acknowledge receipt within 30 days, investigate and tell you the outcome. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or, in the EEA, to a competent supervisory authority, including where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred.

13. International Transfers

We are based in Japan, and our servers are operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Your information may therefore be transferred to and processed in Japan, the United States and other countries where Cloudflare operates. EU and UK adequacy arrangements cover transfers to qualifying recipients in Japan within their scope; they do not automatically cover onward transfers to other countries. For transfers to Cloudflare, we rely on Cloudflare's participation in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, and, for other restricted transfers, on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum in Cloudflare's data processing terms (https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/). Contact us to obtain a copy of the safeguards that apply to your data.

14. California Residents

We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended), and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The categories of personal information we collect, their sources, purposes and retention periods are described in sections 1, 3 and 7. California residents may send privacy requests to nubonba@gmail.com. Where the CCPA applies to us, we will handle those requests in accordance with its requirements. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

15. Language

This Policy is provided in Japanese and English. The Japanese version is the governing text, but it does not override your mandatory rights or reduce any express commitment made to you in this English version.
