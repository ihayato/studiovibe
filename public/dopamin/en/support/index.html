FAQ

Does it cost anything?

The App is free to download. There are no in-app purchases or paid content. We do not show third-party ads (we may show news about our other apps). You are responsible for any data charges from your network provider.

Can I move my data to a new device?

Create a Transfer Code from "Save" on the title screen, then enter it under "Transfer with Code" on the new device. A code can be used only once and expires after 30 days.

What happens if I delete the App?

Your save data is stored on your device, so deleting the App deletes it. Please create a Transfer Code before deleting.

How do I delete my Leaderboard data?

On the Leaderboard screen, choose "Edit Name & Portrait", then "Leave the Leaderboard". For other requests, email us at the address above.
